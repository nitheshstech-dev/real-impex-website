# REAL IMPEX — Corporate Website

> **Weaving Trust Across Borders**

Official website project for **REAL IMPEX**, a home textile manufacturing and export business based in **Karur, Tamil Nadu, India**.

---

## About REAL IMPEX

**REAL IMPEX** is a textile manufacturing and export partner serving buyers across home textiles, hospitality, healthcare and custom-made textile products.

Established in **2007**, the business is built around textile sourcing, product development, manufacturing coordination, quality control and export support. Rather than positioning the website around a single factory, REAL IMPEX presents a flexible manufacturing network capable of matching orders with suitable production partners based on product type, capacity and specialization.

The company's focus is simple: **reliable product development, consistent quality, responsive order management and dependable export execution.**

### Core capabilities

- Home textile manufacturing and sourcing
- Hospitality and hotel linen
- Hospital and healthcare textiles
- Kitchen and dining textiles
- Fabrics and textile materials
- Custom-made-ups
- Private-label manufacturing
- Product development and sampling
- Quality coordination and inspection
- Packaging, documentation and export support

## About This Website

This repository contains the responsive corporate website developed for REAL IMPEX. The website is designed as a **B2B textile buyer-facing platform**, with an emphasis on credibility, product discovery, customization and enquiry generation.

The experience communicates REAL IMPEX as a professional textile partner rather than simply an online product catalogue.

### Website objectives

1. Present REAL IMPEX and its manufacturing model clearly.
2. Help international and domestic buyers discover product categories quickly.
3. Explain customization, fabrics, construction and product-development capabilities.
4. Build trust through transparent and responsible business communication.
5. Generate qualified product and quotation enquiries.
6. Provide downloadable company and business documentation where appropriate.
7. Establish a scalable digital foundation for future CRM and business integrations.

## Trust & Content Accuracy

This project follows a strict rule: **do not publish unsupported business claims.**

The website should not display any GST number, IEC number, certificate number, certification logo, customer logo, testimonial, market count, production capacity or other specific business claim unless it has been supplied from an official and verifiable source.

Where documentation is not yet available, the website uses appropriate placeholder or explanatory language rather than presenting unverified information as fact.

The statutory and certification document index included in the website is an **index/placeholder**, not a replacement for official certificates.
