# REAL IMPEX website research notes — August 2026

The website adds market-facing content without converting research into unsupported company claims.

## Current market signals used in the website
- Sustainable and certified-material stories are increasingly relevant to textile buyers.
- Wellness/performance features such as quick-dry, cooling and soft-touch positioning can differentiate bath and bedding collections where technically applicable.
- Private-label buyers increasingly value customization, smaller test programs, packaging and faster product development.
- Traceability, documentation and supplier visibility are becoming more important in procurement.
- Tactile textures, calm palettes and functional design are useful directions for premium home-textile collections.

## AI operations content
The website frames AI as an operational roadmap rather than claiming that every system is already deployed. Areas include:
- demand and quotation intelligence
- production-risk alerts
- quality analytics
- partner/capability matching
- document intelligence
- buyer communication and operational visibility
